## Questions not answered by a published rule

- The maximum number of BUIDLs one account, individual or team may enter.
- Whether a person may participate in multiple teams or receive multiple awards.
- Whether a second project receives a separate Jam credit allocation.
- Whether repositories must be public or use a specific license.
- Whether the event requires work to begin within a defined competition window.
- Whether two projects may reuse one Mind without violating entry or grant rules.

Absence of a prohibition is not proof of broader permission. Do not overwrite the existing
DealMinder entry. A valid second submission must receive a new BUIDL number and preserve BUIDL
`47665` unchanged.
