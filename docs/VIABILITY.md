# Viability Hypothesis

ContextPatch targets independent creators who sell courses or memberships, or publish sponsored
content across several platforms. Their risk is not a lack of new content; it is leaving stale
prices, dates, access terms or disclosures in older repurposed copies.

## Hypothesized path

1. **Free local audit:** import a small, creator-owned content set; identify affected variants and
   export a human review checklist. No social account connection and no automatic publishing.
2. **Individual workflow:** persistent correction principles, recurring due-work review and a local
   audit trail for one creator.
3. **Team plan hypothesis:** **$9–19 per month** for shared approval roles, multiple content libraries,
   policy history and evidence exports. Exact packaging and price are untested.

## Why someone might pay

- A missed price or date correction can create support work, refund risk or audience confusion.
- Sponsors and regulated offers may require consistent disclosures across old copies.
- A review queue is cheaper to inspect than manually searching every platform after each change.

These are reasonable hypotheses, not validated demand.

## Current commercial evidence

- Real users: **0**
- Paying users: **0**
- Revenue: **$0**
- Refunds and payouts: **none**
- Willingness to pay: **not tested**

No Payhip product, checkout or payment collection is part of this hackathon build. A local demo,
passing test, public repository, video view or BUIDL submission would not count as commercial
validation.

## Cheapest next validation after the hackathon

Interview five independent course, membership or sponsored-content creators using their own recent
fact-change examples. Offer the free local audit first. Only after repeated use, test a manual
invoice or compliant checkout for the proposed team workflow. Do not add publishing integrations
or recurring infrastructure costs before creators demonstrate repeat usage and payment intent.
